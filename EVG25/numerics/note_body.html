plan-EVGQ17-M0, M6 — numerics past the horizon (gate G‑1)

The 2026‑09‑11 readings re-measured with an independent exact program, from \(j\le24\) up to \(j=32\), in three seed windows. Verdict: (a) confirmed; (b) confirmed in normalized form, but “\(\beta_j\) bounded” struck; (c) confirmed, but its “slowly drifting runs” are a feature of the origin window only. Conjecture H\(_1\) keeps its support; the hope that \(|\beta_j|\) does not grow at all does not.

Written 2026‑10‑06 · Parent: plans/plan-EVGQ17-M0.html §0.3 (readings), §3.1–3.2, §5 (M6, G‑1) · Code and data: EVG25/numerics/ (horizon.c, crosscheck.py, analyze.py, make_note.py, data/) · G‑1 exit criterion (verbatim): “readings (a)–(c) of §0.3 confirmed or struck at \(j=28\)–30”.
G‑1 verdict
  • (a) Coin behaviour up to horizon \(3j\): confirmed. Mean, variance and tails of \(S_k\) for \(k\le3j\) agree with \(k\) fair coins to sampling error, in all three windows. One systematic exception, explained by (b): tails just past the horizon are lighter than binomial (\(z=-4.2\) at \(j=30\), \(k=j+7\)).
  • (b) Super-uniform boundary layer: confirmed, normalized. For \(j\ge28\), \(|\beta_j|/(2^{j/2}/2)\le0.045\) in every window, and \(|z_{j,j+t}|\le0.1\) for \(t\le3\). The 8-letter block at position \(j\) has \(\chi^2/\mathrm{df}\) between 0.02 and 0.38 for \(j\ge22\), where a random sample gives \(1\pm0.09\). Struck: “\(|\beta_j|\le173\)” as a sign of boundedness (\(\beta_{25}=-867\), \(\beta_{31}=-795\)); and “all of \(z_{j,j},\dots,z_{j,j+13}\) small” (the layer is about 7 letters wide, and \(z_{27,38}=-2.22\)).
  • (c) Coin scale further out: confirmed. Beyond the layer, rms \(z\) is 0.7–1.1. Struck as general: “slowly drifting runs of one sign”. At \(N_0=0\) the lag-1 autocorrelation of \(z_{j,i}\) is \(+0.4\) to \(+0.6\); in the two generic windows it is between \(-0.2\) and \(+0.2\).

Consequence for M7: H\(_1\) (\(\beta_j=o(2^{j/2})\), or any \(o(2^j)\) bound) is as well supported as before; a fit gives \(|\beta_j|\approx2^{0.08j}\) over \(14\le j\le32\), but nineteen numbers cannot tell slow exponential growth from polynomial growth. The plan’s remark (O1) that “\(|\beta_j|\) may not grow at all” is withdrawn. The pairing identity of §3.2 holds exactly for every \(j\le32\) and in every window, not just at \(N_0=0\).

1 Method

Engine. horizon.c runs over all seeds \(n=N_0+r\), \(0\le r<2^j\), and over letters \(i0.15k\}\), pair counts \(\#\{b_i\ne b_{i+d}\}\) for \(d\le3\), the histogram of the 8-letter block at position \(j\), and the pairing sum of §3.2. Integers are kept mod \(2^{128}\). Since \(U(x)\bmod 2^{a-1}\) depends only on \(x\bmod2^a\), letter \(i\) is exact for \(i<128\). OpenMP, 6 threads, under 2 MB RSS; \(j=32\) (\(4.3\cdot10^9\) seeds, 96 letters) took 286 s.

Independence (R‑5/R‑6). The 2026‑09‑11 script horizon.py is not in the repository, so this is a fresh implementation. It is checked two ways. First, crosscheck.py recomputes every field with Python big integers and the literal definition \(\lfloor3n/2\rfloor+1\), with no modular arithmetic: it matches at \((j,K)=(10,30),(13,39)\), at \(N_0=10^{15}+12345\) and at \(N_0=\lfloor\sqrt2\cdot10^{37}\rfloor\) (\(j=12\)), and at \(N_0\approx2^{128}\) with \(K=120\), the wrap-around limit. Second, all eleven published \(\beta_j\) (\(14\le j\le24\)) are reproduced exactly. As a further sanity check, for \(k\le j\) every letter count equals \(2^{j-1}\) and every tail fraction equals the binomial tail exactly, as Theorem 1 (EVG25.M0.uPrefix_bijective) requires.

Windows. \(N_0=0\) for \(8\le j\le32\); \(N_0=10^{15}+12345\) (the plan’s offset) and \(N_0=\lfloor\sqrt2\cdot10^{37}\rfloor=14142135623730950488016887242096980785\) for even \(j\) in \(14..28\) and \(14..30\). A first choice \(N_0=2^{100}+277\) was discarded: \(N_0\equiv277\pmod{2^{3j+1}}\), so it is the origin window shifted by 277 and not independent.

Normalization. \(z_{j,i}=(\#\{r:b_i\text{ odd}\}-2^{j-1})/(2^{j/2}/2)\), which is \(N(0,1)\) for \(2^j\) independent fair coins; \(\beta_j\) is the numerator at \(i=j\), and \(t=i-j\) is the distance past the horizon.

2 The first letter past the horizon

\(\beta_j/(2^{j/2}/2)\) in three windows; shaded \(\pm1\sigma\), \(\pm2\sigma\) for fair coins. From \(j=20\) on the points stay inside \(\pm0.3\); from \(j=28\) inside \(\pm0.05\).

The ratio to coin scale keeps shrinking, so super-uniformity of the first letter persists to \(j=32\). The absolute size does not stay bounded: \(|\beta_j|\) jumps from 63 at \(j=24\) to 867 at \(j=25\) and stays in the hundreds, with \(\beta_{32}=9\). The least-squares slope of \(\log_2|\beta_j|\) on \(14\le j\le32\) is 0.077. That is far below the coin exponent \(\frac12\) and also far below the \(\theta<\frac12\) that O1 targets; but with this many points it supports no exponent claim.

3 The boundary layer

\(z_{j,j+t}\) for \(t=0..13\). Green: \(|z|\le0.1\). Amber: \(|z|\ge1\).

@T3@

The layer of strongly suppressed bias is about seven letters wide for \(j\ge28\), in both windows, and inside it \(|z|\) shrinks with \(j\) (\(\le0.05\) for \(t\le3\) at \(j=30\)–32). After it, the bias is at coin scale within a few letters. The 2026‑09‑11 observation that all fourteen values were small at \(j=24\) was a coincidence of that \(j\): at \(j=26\)–28 the window \(t\le13\) already reaches \(|z|\approx1\)–2.

5 Exact binomial counts for Theorem 2

6 Pairing identity

The §3.2 identity \(\beta_j=\sum_{r<2^{j-1},\,b_{j-1}(r)=[j\text{ odd}]}(2b_j(r)-1)\) holds exactly in every run: \(8\le j\le32\) at \(N_0=0\) (previously checked to \(j\le18\)), and at both offsets. That is expected, since F7 holds at every base point; the plan states it only at the origin and should say so in general.
