\chapter{Lean dictionary}\label{app:dictionary}

Each chapter ends with a table from its statements to Lean declarations. This appendix gives
the coarser map from the Lean files of \texttt{CarryGraph/} to the chapters, and then the bridge
root \texttt{CarryBridge/}: every certificate of the corpus, the carry-graph property it
witnesses, and the root theorems re-derived from the generic ones (primed names, same
statements). Every theorem in both roots uses only the axioms \lean{propext},
\lean{Classical.choice} and \lean{Quot.sound}; the files \texttt{AxCheck.lean} in each root
print them (464 theorems in \texttt{CarryGraph/}; 173 in \texttt{CarryBridge/}, of which the 11 of
\texttt{Z32Balance} are printed by \texttt{AxCheckBalance.lean}, because \texttt{Z32.BalanceVectors}
and \texttt{Z32.CertComplete} cannot be imported together).
